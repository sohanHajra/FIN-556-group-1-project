VenueArb Strategy

Overview
--------
VenueArb is a minimal, event-driven venue arbitrage strategy that exploits
top-of-book price discrepancies between NASDAQ and IEX. The strategy reacts
to direct quote updates and maintains a target position based on cross-venue
bid/ask relationships.

Signal Logic
------------
For each instrument, the strategy continuously tracks the latest bid and ask
from both NASDAQ and IEX.

A trading signal is generated as follows:

- Go LONG if:
    IEX bid > NASDAQ ask + arb_threshold

- Go SHORT if:
    NASDAQ bid > IEX ask + arb_threshold

- Otherwise:
    Target position is FLAT

The arb_threshold parameter is used to filter out noise and microprice
fluctuations.

Execution Logic
---------------
Orders are routed to NASDAQ only. The strategy maintains at most one working
order per instrument at any time.

Orders are placed as limit orders at the current top-of-book price, adjusted
by the aggressiveness parameter:

- Buy orders: ask - aggressiveness
- Sell orders: bid + aggressiveness

Position Management
-------------------
The strategy compares the desired target position to the current portfolio
position and submits orders only when an adjustment is required. Positions
are automatically reduced or flattened when the arbitrage condition no
longer holds.
